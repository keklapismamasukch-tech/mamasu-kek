Paste the WhatsApp list as it is. Cakes under KEK PREMIUM (or BUTTER) go to Butter bakers, cakes under KEK REGULAR go to Regular bakers. Baker names, headings and the Adunan lines below the ____ line are skipped.

Bakers

Untick anyone who is off today. The number is the priority: bakers at the top get cakes first. Tap ▲ to move someone up. Tap BUTTER / REGULAR to move a baker to the other group. Butter bakers with no butter cakes can help with Regular; Regular bakers never get butter cakes.

Share with all phones

Paste your Google Sheet sync link (ends with /exec) so every phone uses the same bakers, cake levels and history. Each phone only needs this once, or put it in the HTML file and nobody needs to.

Cakes per baker

Per baker: how many cakes one baker should get, so only as many bakers as needed are used (18 cakes ÷ 9 = 2 bakers). Max is the most a baker can take; Buffer is used only when everyone is already at Max. Above the buffer, the page warns New Bakers Needed.

Fair pay

Every day you tap Copy & Save, the split is saved in the Google Sheet. When choosing who bakes, bakers with the fewest cakes so far go first, so everyone ends up with about the same amount. See the totals by tapping the date on the Generate tab.

Cake list & level

Tap the level to switch it: HARD → MEDIUM → EASY. Tap the class to switch BUTTER ↔ REGULAR. The class decides who bakes a cake when the pasted list has no PREMIUM / REGULAR heading.

Remembered corrections

When you correct a scanned cake, the scanner remembers that spelling for next time.
